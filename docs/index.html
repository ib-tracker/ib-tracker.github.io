Why this exists

I'm going into DP2. I built this for myself first.

My exams are in May 2027. Before I wrote this, my whole system was Stickies on my Mac. I'd note a task down and then forget it existed. Grades lived somewhere else, university options somewhere else again, and most deadlines were just in my head.

I wanted one place that held all of it, and that already understood the Diploma without me having to translate it first.

I also live about an hour from school, each way. That's two hours a day I am never going to study in, so the scheduler counts travel time around everything and only plans into what's actually left. The estimate accuracy chart came from the same place. I kept giving a task an hour when it really wanted two, and I wanted to see how wrong I was.

Everything stays on my laptop, so it works with no Wi-Fi and my grades aren't sitting on anyone else's computer.

I use it every day for my own coursework, which is the only reason it keeps getting better. When something annoys me while I'm using it, it usually gets fixed that week.

After you download

Opening it the first time

macOS will refuse to open it and say Apple “cannot check it for malicious software”. That means the app hasn't been through Apple's paid notarisation service. Here's the way through, and you only do it once.

  1. Drag IB Tracker to Applications, then double-click it. It'll be blocked. That's expected, and it's the step that makes the next one possible, so don't skip it.
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll to the bottom, to Security. You'll see “IB Tracker was blocked to protect your Mac”. Click Open Anyway.
  4. Confirm with Touch ID or your password, then click Open.

After that it opens normally, and updates install without asking again. On macOS Sequoia and later, Control-clicking the app and choosing Open no longer works, because Apple removed that shortcut. System Settings is the only route.

Questions

Can I try it before paying?

Yes. Download it and pick “try it with sample data” on the first screen, which gives you a realistic set of tasks, grades and study sessions to click through. You can clear it any time in Settings.

What exactly stops working without a key?

Adding and changing things. Reading, searching, filtering, the analytics, progress reports and exporting all keep working. If a key runs out, nothing is deleted and you can still take everything with you.

Does it work offline?

Completely. There is no server. The only network request the app ever makes is a once-a-day version check, and that can be turned off in Settings.

Will it run on my Mac?

If it's an M1 or newer, yes. That's every Mac sold since about 2020, and every Mac sold new today.

Not sure? Open the Apple menu in the top-left corner of your screen and choose About This Mac. If the Chip line says M1, M2, M3, M4 or similar, you're fine. If it says Intel, this won't run.

It also needs one extra step the first time you open it, because it isn't distributed through the App Store. The steps are in opening it the first time.

Is the coach an AI? Does it send my data anywhere?

The built-in coach runs entirely on your machine and sends nothing. If you want full AI conversations you can add your own OpenRouter API key, and only then is your study data sent to OpenRouter when you chat. The key is stored locally and is never included in backups.

Can I move to a new laptop?

Export a backup from Settings, install the app on the new Mac, and import it. Your licence key is not stored in the backup file, so keep the email it came in and enter it again in Settings.

What about Windows or iPad?

Not yet, this is a Mac app today. The core also runs as a plain web page in any browser, but the Mac app is the supported way to use it.
