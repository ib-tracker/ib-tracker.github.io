IB Study Tracker

Tasks, scheduling, grades, analytics and a study coach — all in one app that runs entirely on your own computer. No account, no internet, no setup.

Download for macOS

Open the .dmg, drag IB Tracker to Applications, and you're done.

Download the latest release
First launch: macOS may say the app is from an unidentified developer. Right-click the app → Open → Open. This only happens once.

Your data

  • Everything is saved on this computer and never leaves it.
  • The app updates itself — it checks once a day and asks before installing.
  • An update only replaces the app's own files. Your tasks, sessions, grades and settings are untouched.
  • Take a backup any time from Settings → Export backup.
Editing needs a licence key, sold as prepaid blocks — nothing auto-renews. Without one the app is read-only: everything can still be read, searched, reported on and exported, so your work is always yours to take with you.
